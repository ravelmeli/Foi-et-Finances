# Prompts Nano Banana — Short « Besoin ou envie selon la Bible »

Autocollants déjà prêts dans `ressources/kit-chretien/` : Bible fermée et ouverte, pupitre de la Parole, piédestal de pièces, cœur, croix verte, bonhomme (prière, lecture, pensif, tiraillé, soucieux, serein).
Il ne manque qu'une planche d'objets « besoin / envie » et quatre poses.

## PLANCHE A — objets besoin / envie (6 éléments)
Ordre de découpe : `panier-besoins`, `maison-simple`, `vetements-plies`, `boite-smartphone`, `montre-luxe`, `sac-shopping`.

```
Clean minimalist hand-drawn 2D illustration in a clear-line stickman explainer style, thick clean black outlines, flat soft colors, light pastel blue and soft green accents, warm golden coins, plenty of white space. Pure white background, 2048 x 2048 square. Six separate objects arranged in a 3 x 2 grid with generous spacing, each one isolated with no overlap and no background behind it: top left, a woven grocery basket with bread, an apple, a bottle of milk and some vegetables; top middle, a small simple house with a light blue roof, a door and two windows; top right, a neat pile of folded clothes, a sweater and trousers; bottom left, a brand-new smartphone in an open premium white box, shiny; bottom middle, a luxury golden wristwatch with sparkles; bottom right, a stylish paper shopping bag with rope handles and a ribbon. No text, no letters, no numbers, no writing, no dollar signs, no logos, no brands, no watermark, no shadows on the background, no photorealism.
```
